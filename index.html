03 — The Money

How we
make money

Two legs. One pays in big project spikes, one pays every month. Neither involves us installing anything — or selling raw leads.

Leg 1 · Project money

Coordination margin

We source the construction project, line up the installer as bidder of record, and coordinate scope, quotes and scheduling so nothing falls through. When the partner wins, we take an agreed margin:

Agreed rates — Doug 3%, Jerry 5%, bench 3–5% estimates — set on a call, before bid day.

We are never the GC. We never sell raw leads. The partner bids in their own name and does the work in their own name.
