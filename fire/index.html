1. The bid engine (the backbone)

An hourly sweep of NC / SC / GA procurement (NC eVP, state registries, BidNet, Bonfire, OpenGov), GC and CM portals, SAM.gov and plan rooms hunts Division 21 sprinkler, fire alarm and NFPA 25 inspection work across three tiers: Charlotte metro first, then NC/SC statewide, then Georgia. Every opportunity is diligence-checked before it is routed — live at the official source from its own fields, procurement stage verified, prequal and mandatory-meeting eligibility checked, and scope fit confirmed against the partner's stated capabilities and license. Anything unverified stays stored and flagged, never presented as a clean pursuit.

3. The recurring layer (retention engine)

NFPA 25 sprinkler inspections, annual fire alarm testing, backflow testing and correction work after failed inspections are recurring revenue — they count double in planning toward the $10,000/month target and are the low-churn core of the line. The City of South Fulton inspection contract and the Savannah-Chatham schools alarm monitoring/testing contract are the shape of what we hunt.
