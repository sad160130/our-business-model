How the fire model works

1 · Bid engine

Hourly sweep of NC/SC/GA procurement, GC portals, SAM.gov and plan rooms for Div 21 sprinkler, fire alarm and NFPA 25 inspection work; every opportunity diligence-checked (live at official source, stage, eligibility, scope + license fit) before routing, and unverified items stay flagged.

2 · Outreach

Tim-voice email coordination — we help owners, GCs and facilities find the right licensed fire contractor and coordinate scope, quotes and scheduling; all drafts are staged for Sanket to send.

3 · Recurring layer

NFPA 25 inspections, annual alarm testing and correction work are the retention engine — recurring contracts count double toward the $10,000/month target.

Money model: partners pay $2,500/month + 3–5% commission on wins, in writing (Partner Coordination Agreement) before bid day; the partner is always bidder of record.
